why we exist

Technology keeps us online. We'd rather it keep us connected.

Feeds, algorithms, and apps are tuned to hold attention, not build relationships. We build ideas that make people more human, not less.

Some of our ideas will be small and short-lived. Some will last for years. All of them push community and connection forward, and all of them run through the same character, a fwend.

See one and you know something authentic happened there: a memory made with someone else, not alone. The goal isn't for Fwends to just be a brand people recognize. It's for the character to become synonymous with connection itself

our first idea: the fwends app

A digital world fueled by real social connection.

Fwends is a new genre of game where your world grows through the connections you make. Share something meaningful in the app, meet up in person, and each moment feeds your fwend and builds the world it lives in.
